# Nepal Geospatial Legislation Archive
The Geospatial Legislation Archive centralizes Nepal's geospatial and land laws, plus international references. It houses policies, acts, standards, circulars, and drafts to guide policy and legislative development.

**OVERVIEW**

The Geospatial Legislation Archive is a centralized repository for geospatial and land management legislation. Focused primarily on Nepal alongside key international reference materials, it encompasses policies, acts, regulations, technical standards, circulars, and related legal frameworks. In addition to enacted laws, the archive hosts draft legislation to support policy development and guide the legislative process.

**OBJECTIVES**

The Archive serves as a unified digital portal offering open access to Nepal's geospatial laws, policies, and technical standards alongside relevant international legislation.

Specific objectives include:
1. Centralized Access: Consolidating legislation currently scattered across various individual government websites into a single searchable repository.

2. Historical Preservation: Archiving historical and superseded regulatory documents, which are frequently removed from official websites when updated, ensuring ongoing access for legal, academic, and technical research.

**FEATURES**
1. Open Access & Hosting: The repository is fully open to the public and hosted transparently on GitHub, ensuring long-term accessibility and reliable version control.
2. Searchability & Navigation: Built using MkDocs, the portal provides a fast, responsive interface with built-in full-text search across all indexed regulatory documents.
